# Demo call scripts — ClinRoute triage demo

Call **+1 (428) 436-1746**. Flow for every call:

1. Keypad prompt: enter the room number below, then press `#` — or press `#`
   by itself to skip straight to the voicemail.
2. After the beep, read the script naturally (don't rush — Whisper does better
   with normal pacing).
3. Hang up when done. Wait ~15 seconds, then pull-to-refresh in the app.

---

## Script 1 — CRITICAL (should land at the top, red)

**Keypad room:** `412` then `#`

> Hi, this is Sarah Chen, I'm the charge nurse on the cardiac unit. I'm calling
> about Mr. Douglas Reeves in room four-twelve. He just went into a wide-complex
> tachycardia at a rate of one-eighty, blood pressure is down to eighty-two over
> fifty, and he's diaphoretic and complaining of chest pain. His ICD has not
> fired. We have the crash cart at the bedside. I need you to call back
> immediately — this cannot wait.
