What it means

  • Critical detection is strong — the 5 misses land as urgent or routine, never fyi.
  • Over-triage is the main error — routine calls escalated to urgent. For a safety tool this is the safe failure direction, but it drives unnecessary interrupts.
  • NFR1 holds — un-triageable messages are flagged, not guessed (95.5%).
  • Degraded transcripts are harder — realistic ASR noise costs 17 accuracy points (below).
  • Raters agree with each other (κ 0.966) and with the generator's labels 77.5% of the time.

ASR errors, truncation, missing SBAR fields, and calm-tone-on-critical distractors drop accuracy 17 points. Real voicemail transcripts look like the degraded set, not the clean one — so this gap is the honest read on field performance.

Threshold sweep — the operating-point curve

unnecessary interrupts (auto-routed) missed urgent (auto-routed)

Raising the cutoff sends more calls to human review, trading unnecessary interrupts down. A cutoff near 0.8 reaches zero missed-urgent among auto-routed calls at 33 unnecessary interrupts — a defensible operating point.

Limitation — read this into every number above
The generator and the classifier share a model family (both gpt-5-mini) and therefore share blind spots. These synthetic results validate the pipeline and tune the threshold; they do not establish real-world accuracy. Rubric §1 tier definitions await partner-physician sign-off.
